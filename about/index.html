About

A public reading room for a personal library.

Thesarium is a collection of things I wanted to understand more deeply—history, science, technology, culture, and the useful arts.

Some entries are researched essays. Others are notes gathered along the way. The goal is simple: learn something worth knowing, preserve it, and make it useful to someone else.

This is a collection in progress rather than a claim to authority. Sources and further reading belong beside conclusions; questions and unfinished thoughts have a place here too.

“To collect is to pay attention to the world.”
